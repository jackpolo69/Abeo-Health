## 3. Description of Services

### 3.1 Abeó Care (Non-Medical Home Care) — planned service, offered only when live on the site
When live, Abeó Care will provide non-medical home care services delivered by Home Care Aides (HCAs) registered on the California Home Care Aide Registry (HCAR), in accordance with the client's individualized care plan. Services may include:
- Personal care assistance (bathing, dressing, grooming, hygiene, mobility)
- Companionship and meaningful daily engagement
- Meal preparation per dietary needs
- Medication reminders (non-administration; HCAs do not administer medications)
- Light housekeeping and laundry
- Errands and transportation to appointments (when appropriate, in coordination with Abeó Transport)
- Activities of daily living support

**What Abeó Care will NOT provide in Phase 1:**
- Skilled nursing care (Phase 3 service via separate CDPH HHA license)
- Physical, occupational, or speech therapy (Phase 3)
- Medication administration (requires clinical licensure — Phase 3)
- Diagnosis, treatment, or management of medical conditions (we are not a medical practice)
- Emergency medical services (call 911 for medical emergencies)

### 3.2 Abeó Connect (Care Coordination) — planned service, offered only when live on the site
When live, Abeó Connect will provide a dedicated coordinator and family-facing dashboard to manage the client's care journey, including: scheduling, family communication, vendor coordination, and senior placement guidance when home is not the right destination.

### 3.3 Service Pricing
Published pricing is available on the abeo.health website. Pricing is subject to change with reasonable notice. Specific pricing for the client's care plan will be documented in the Service Agreement signed at intake.

## 5. Caregiver Standards

### 5.1 Home Care Aide Qualifications
All Abeó Home Care Aides are:
- W-2 employees of Abeó Health, LLC (not independent contractors)
- Registered on the California Home Care Aide Registry (HCAR) before any client contact
- Subject to DOJ and FBI criminal background checks
- TB-clearance current
- Trained per Abeó's Training Plan
- Insured under Abeó's general liability and workers compensation policies
- Bound by Abeó's Personnel Policies

### 5.2 Scope of Practice
Caregivers operate within the scope of non-medical home care. They may not:
- Administer medications (only remind)
- Provide skilled nursing services
- Operate medical equipment requiring clinical training
- Make medical decisions on behalf of the client
- Provide services outside the agreed care plan without supervisor approval

### 5.3 Caregiver-Client Boundaries
Clients agree not to:
- Request services outside the agreed care plan or caregiver scope
- Offer or provide gratuities or gifts in excess of $25 per occasion (per CDSS guidance)
- Engage caregivers privately outside Abeó (i.e., no "side deals" — caregivers are Abeó employees and may not contract directly with clients during their employment with us)
- Discriminate against caregivers based on protected categories (race, religion, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, disability, etc.)
