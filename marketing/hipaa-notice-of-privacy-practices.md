# Abeó Health — HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

**THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.**

**Effective Date:** July 26, 2026
**Owner:** Abeó Health, LLC, a California limited liability company

---

## Our Commitment

Abeó Health, LLC is required by law to maintain the privacy of your protected health information (PHI), to provide you with this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and to follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect.

## How We May Use and Disclose Your PHI

- **Treatment** — to provide and coordinate your care among your providers and caregivers.
- **Payment** — to bill and obtain payment for the services you receive.
- **Health Care Operations** — for quality, training, and administration.
- **As Required or Permitted by Law** — for example, public health activities, reporting abuse or neglect, court orders, and to avert a serious threat to health or safety.

Other uses and disclosures — including most marketing, any sale of PHI, and disclosure of psychotherapy notes — require your written authorization, which you may revoke in writing at any time.

## Your Rights

- **Access** — inspect and obtain a copy of your PHI.
- **Amendment** — request correction of PHI you believe is incorrect or incomplete.
- **Accounting of Disclosures** — request a list of certain disclosures we have made.
- **Restrictions** — request limits on uses and disclosures, including to a health plan for services you paid for in full out of pocket.
- **Confidential Communications** — request that we contact you by alternative means or at an alternative location.
- **Breach Notification** — be notified if your unsecured PHI is breached.
- **Paper Copy** — obtain a paper copy of this Notice on request, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.

## Our Duties

We are required to protect the privacy of your PHI, provide this Notice, notify you following a breach of unsecured PHI, and abide by the terms of the Notice currently in effect. We reserve the right to change this Notice and to make the revised Notice effective for PHI we already have, as well as information we receive in the future.

## Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with us or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights (200 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, D.C. 20201). **We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.**
